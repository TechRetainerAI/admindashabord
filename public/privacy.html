MeDan Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026

MeDan ("we", "us") operates the MeDan mobile application and related services that help students in Ghana find and book verified hostel accommodation. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you register we collect your name, email address, password (stored only as a cryptographic hash), and optionally your phone number.

Student profile. You may optionally provide your campus, course, department, level, index number, a profile photo, and an emergency contact (parent or guardian name, phone number and relationship). This helps hostel managers confirm student bookings and reach a contact in an emergency.

Booking and payment information. When you book a room we record the booking details (hostel, room, amount, dates and status). Payments are processed by Paystack; we receive payment references and status but never see or store your full card number or mobile money PIN.

Device information. With your permission we store a push notification token for your device so we can send you booking updates.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account (including emailed verification codes).
  • To process bookings and hold payments in escrow until you confirm move-in.
  • To send booking, payment and check-in notifications.
  • To let hostel managers verify student bookings.
  • To provide customer support and resolve disputes.

3. Sharing

We share booking details (your name, programme and contact information) with the manager of the hostel you book. Payments are processed by Paystack. Push notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us (below); we will delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep for financial or legal reasons.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords and verification codes are stored only as salted cryptographic hashes.

6. Children

MeDan is intended for university and college students aged 16 and over.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above always reflects the latest version.
